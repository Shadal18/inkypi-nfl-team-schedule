🏈 NFL team schedule

Show a team’s next kickoff or most recent game.

Pick an NFL team and optionally provide a display title. InkyPi shows today’s game when available, the next scheduled matchup when the team is idle, or the most recently completed game as a fallback.

📅

Automatic game selection

No API key is required. The plugin automatically prioritizes today’s game, then the next upcoming matchup, then the most recent finished game when no upcoming game is available.

🏈

Matchup settings

Choose the team and optional title shown on the display.

Select the team whose upcoming schedule and latest results should be displayed.

Optional. Leave blank to use the generated matchup title automatically.
